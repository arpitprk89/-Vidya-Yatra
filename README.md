# 🇮🇳 Vidya Yatra — The Journey of Learning

**How India's students study, from Grade 1 to 12, and what happens next.**

An independent research website by **[Arpit Pareek](https://in.linkedin.com/in/arpit-pareek-83b533361)** exploring how Indian students study at each school stage, how they choose a stream in Class 11, and how those choices shape their futures.

`Independent Research • Open to Correction`

---

## Why this project

India has one of the largest school systems in the world, yet outsiders often struggle to understand it: the stream system, the board exams, the entrance-exam culture and the coaching industry. This project explains that system in plain English and asks how study habits formed in school connect to later life outcomes.

## What the site covers

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **The Journey** | The four NEP 2020 school stages (Foundational, Preparatory, Middle, Secondary) and how students study in each |
| **The Foundation Gap** | Learning data from ASER 2024 showing how weak foundations travel upward |
| **Every Stream, Honestly** | Science (PCM), Science (PCB), Commerce, Humanities, Vocational and the NEP flexible mix: how each studies, where it leads and its hidden costs |
| **What Changes After School** | The forks at Class 10, Class 12, college and work |
| **Who Won, and How** | Documented Indian journeys, with a note on survivorship bias |
| **The System** | An interactive diagram of the forces shaping a student: family, school, coaching, exams, peers, phone |
| **Find Your Study Pattern** | A short interactive reflection quiz (nothing is stored or sent) |
| **Glossary** | Definitions of Indian education terms for international readers |
| **Method & Limits** | What is data, what is a model, and what is unverified |

## Data and honesty

- Figures labelled **DATA** come from **ASER 2024** (Pratham Foundation), a household survey of **rural** India. They do not describe urban students.
- Stage and stream descriptions are a **research model** built from commonly discussed patterns. They are hypotheses to test, not measured facts.
- The site does not give career advice and does not replace a school counsellor.
- Future work: a real survey (students, parents, teachers) and state-wise comparison.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages

1. Create a new repository and upload `index.html` and `README.md`.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
4. Your site will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
